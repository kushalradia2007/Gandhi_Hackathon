CAFE MANAGER 98  v1.3 (shareware - registered to SHREE CYBER ZONE)

Manages up to 12 client PCs. Billing is per-second, rounded up to the
next half-hour, because that is how business works.

Known issues:
 - Session on PC 03 cannot be ended from the console.
   Workaround: none. Do not attempt.
 - Billing counter on PC 03 may exceed display width.
 - Printing a bill on PC 12 may cause the printer to "jam".
   This is a feature of the printer, not the software.
