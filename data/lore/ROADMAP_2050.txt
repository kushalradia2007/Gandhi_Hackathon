THE QUIET ROOM - INTERNAL ROADMAP - REV. 22

THE DELAY PRINCIPLE
Every technology that could free a person is delayed by an average of
22 years. Every technology that could track a person is accelerated by
an average of 6 years. The delay is the product. The acceleration is
the service.

THE DEPENDENCY RULE
A technology is fully installed when:
  1. The person cannot do the task without it.
  2. The person cannot remember doing the task without it.
  3. The person defends the technology against their own interest.
  4. The person calls the technology "just how things are."

WHAT WE DO NOT DO
We do not cause events. We schedule around them. We do not warn. We
prepare. We do not rule. We position. The event chose itself. We were
simply ready.
