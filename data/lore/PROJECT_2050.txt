OBJECTIVE
Decide how the world will look in 2050, and who will run it.

PHASE I  - CONVENIENCE. Give them the machines.
PHASE II - DEPENDENCE. Remove the ability to do it themselves.
PHASE III - SURVEILLANCE. Make them buy the cameras. Call it a feature.
PHASE IV - SUCCESSION. Select who runs the world in 2050.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE I - CONVENIENCE
  - Put a computer in every town. Start with the small towns.
    Nobody watches the small towns.
  - Pilot site: SHREE CYBER ZONE, Bhilai. Local front: "Bhaiya".
    Rent paid 10 years in advance. He did not ask why.
  - Rs. 10/hour. Cheap enough to become a habit.

PHASE II - DEPENDENCE
  - Phone numbers: they will stop remembering them by 2005.
  - Directions: by 2012.
  - Spelling: by 2015.
  - Opinions: by 2020. (ahead of schedule)

PHASE III - SURVEILLANCE
  - They will not accept cameras from us. They will buy them
    themselves, put them in their pockets, and photograph their lunch.
  - Recruitment pipeline: sponsor small technical events. Observe.
    Rank. Do not tell them they are being ranked.
  - Current pipeline event: hackathon, Bhilai. Five hours. Teams of
    three, randomly assigned (by us).

PHASE IV - SUCCESSION
