GTA = GENERATIONAL TRAJECTORY ADJUSTMENT.

Not Grand Theft Auto. The game was a cover story. The protocol was
formalized in 1998 under the cover name, because nobody audits a
video game.

G - GENERATIONAL
    Each generation is captured by a different product. What works
    on one generation is embarrassing to the next. This is fine.
    We make the next one too.

T - TRAJECTORY
    The product directs the player's life path. Not by force. By
    scheduling. The player decides what to do with their evenings
    for ten years and believes the decision was theirs.

A - ADJUSTMENT
    The path is adjusted to match the committee's needs. Most
    players are adjusted toward comfort. A few are adjusted toward
    the room.

THE FIVE PHASES

1. AWARENESS.  The player hears about the game.
2. DESIRE.     The player wants the game.
3. CAPTURE.    The player buys the game.
4. RETENTION.  The player cannot stop playing.
5. SUCCESSION. The player becomes the next generation.

Phase 5 is the only phase that matters. Phases 1 through 4 are
marketing.
