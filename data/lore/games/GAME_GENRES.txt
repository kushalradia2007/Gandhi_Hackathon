Every genre has a function. Every function has a target.
The player does not choose a genre. The genre chooses the player.

GENRE                     FUNCTION    TARGET
------------------------  ----------  -----------------------
Open-world crime          Autonomy    Talented teenagers
Fantasy violence          Power       Angry teenagers
Strategy / empire         Control     Ambitious teenagers
Pastoral / farming        Patience    Escapist teenagers
Competitive multiplayer   Status      Social teenagers
Sandbox / creative        Identity    Lonely teenagers

NOTES ON THE INSTALLED GAMES (PC 03)
- Solitaire: patience. The player moves cards from one place to
  another until the cards are in order. Then the cards fly away
  and the player deals again.
- Minesweeper: control. The player clears a field one square at
  a time and is rewarded for never making a mistake.
- Hearts: community. Not available. The player is the only one.
- FreeCell: hope. Deal #11982 cannot be solved. It is left on the
  machine as a reminder.

The sandbox is the most dangerous.
The player who builds a world in a game does not build one
outside it.
