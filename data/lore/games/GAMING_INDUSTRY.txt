SUMMARY
The most dangerous person in the world is a talented teenager
with a goal.

A talented teenager with a goal will build something. Nobody can
predict what. Nobody can schedule it. Unscheduled things are the
only things this committee is afraid of.

The solution is not to remove the talent. Talent is useful later.
The solution is to give the goal somewhere else to go.

THE FOUR PILLARS OF A DISTRACTION PRODUCT

1. PROGRESS.
   Give them a bar to fill. The bar must always be almost full.
   When it is full, give them a longer bar.

2. IDENTITY.
   Let them choose who they are. A class, a colour, a name, a
   hat. Once they have chosen, they will defend the choice.

3. COMMUNITY.
   Give them a group that needs them. The group must meet at a
   fixed time. The fixed time must be inconvenient for everything
   else.

4. FEAR OF LEAVING.
   Make them feel that leaving is a betrayal. Of the group. Of the
   bar. Of the person they chose to be.

FUNDING NOTE (1998)
The committee approved the budget in one meeting. Nobody asked
what the games would be about. The games can be about anything.
The pillars are what matter.

AUDIT NOTE (2019)
Every generation since 1998 has asked the same question:
"Is it bad for them?"

They got what they paid for. They paid for the wrong question.
