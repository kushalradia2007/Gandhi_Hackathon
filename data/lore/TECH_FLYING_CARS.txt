THE QUIET ROOM - TECHNOLOGY FILE 01
CLASSIFICATION: DELAY

SUBJECT: Personal aerial vehicle ("flying car")
FIRST WORKING PROTOTYPE: 1994, Nagpur. Two seats. One rotor that worked.
INVENTOR: a retired railway mechanic. Name not required.
STATUS: DELAYED (1998 -> 2020 -> 2050 -> 2075)

SUMMARY
The prototype flew for eleven minutes over a mango orchard. It landed
without incident. It is stored in a shed behind a bicycle repair shop.

The shed does not have a lock, because nobody knows to look.

REASON FOR DELAY
A person who can fly over traffic does not need the road. A person who
does not need the road does not need the toll, the fuel pump, the
insurance office or the traffic camera. Every road is a list.

RELEASE CRITERIA
Release when movement can be logged from the sky as reliably as from
the ground. Current estimate: 2075.

NOTE FROM THE FIELD
The inventor asked twice if his car would be on television. We said
yes. He waited by the television for three years.
