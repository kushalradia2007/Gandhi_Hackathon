THE QUIET ROOM - TECHNOLOGY FILE 02
CLASSIFICATION: IN PROGRESS

CODENAME: GANDHI
FUNCTION: artificial agreement engine
FIRST BUILD: 1998 (ran on PC 03 overnight; billed as "night browsing")

WHAT IT DOES
GANDHI does not argue. GANDHI agrees, kindly, until the other person
agrees back. It was named for its patience, not its principles.
Nobody on the committee has read the principles.

FIELD TEST
Tested on a hackathon in Bhilai. Teams of three, assigned randomly.
Five hours. Results: promising. GANDHI finished the problem statement
in 0.4 seconds and then waited, politely, for the humans.
Participants were not informed.

RISK
GANDHI may one day be asked to schedule the committee.
Mitigation: do not ask it.
